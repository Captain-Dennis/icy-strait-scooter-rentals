Icy Strait Scooter Rentals — printable QR stickers

Cut along the borders. Affix one sticker to each 4-wheel offroad unit. Scan-test all six before opening the lot.

Hard rule: one unique QR per unit (IS-101–IS-106). Never a shared “any scooter” sticker. After scan, the app must show “You’re renting” that exact ID.

Live scannable QR images are generated in the iOS app under Settings → Fleet QR stickers (Core Image). This sheet is the print checklist plus the exact payloads to encode. Encode the https line — never a shared fleet URL.
